- class `Page`: One schematic sheet and its drawing API. Schematic is the root page of a design; Schematic.sheet() adds more pages.

  - `.add(ref, lib_id, pos, value=None, footprint=None, nets=None, fields=None, rot=0, mirror=False, in_bom=True, on_board=True, dnp=False, unit=1, ref_at=None, val_at=None, hide_value=False, show=None)`: Place a symbol at pos with rotation rot (0/90/180/270, CCW on screen).

  - `.wire(*points)`: Polyline through pins (('R1', 2)) and/or points ((x, y)); segments must be orthogonal.
  - `.link(a, b, first='h')`: Connect two pins/points with one or two orthogonal segments (h: horizontal first).

  - `.power_flag(net, at, length=2.54, direction='up')`: PWR_FLAG on a pin or point, on a short wire in `direction` from it.

  - `.overlaps(design=True)`: List of human-readable text collisions (fields, labels, notes vs everything) in the whole design; collisions on a sheet are prefixed with its name.
  - `.netlist()`: The declared nets of the whole design, all sheets: {net: [(ref, pin), ...]}.
  - `.write_lib_tables(directory, version_var='KICAD10', fp_dirs=())`: Write sym-lib-table and fp-lib-table for the libraries the design uses.

## `leiterbahn.kicad.verify`

ERC plus netlist proof: KiCad's connectivity must equal the nets the generator declared.

- `erc_and_parity(sch, declared_json, build_dir, netlist_name=None)`: Run ERC on sch, export KiCad's netlist into build_dir and compare it with the nets the schematic generator declared (Schematic.netlist() saved as JSON). Returns (erc_summary_line, netlist_path, diffs); diffs is empty when they match.

  - `.place(ref, x, y, rot=0, back=False)`: Fixed placement (marks the part as placed for later place_near calls).
  - `.fits(ref, edge=0.3)`: True if ref's courtyard is inside the board (with edge margin), clear of cut-outs and clear of all other courtyards.
  - `.place_near(ref, x, y, rots=(0, 90, 180, 270), step=0.25, max_r=20.0, edge=0.3, back=False)`: Put ref at the free spot closest to (x, y), trying the given rotations at each radius; back=True puts it on the back of the board.

## `leiterbahn.kicad.place`

Placement by connections, on both board sides.

- `passive(ref)`: True for resistors and capacitors (R12, C3): the parts placed last, on either side.
- `with_lanes(box, pads, lane)`: The box (x0, y0, x1, y1) of a part, grown so that it reaches `lane` mm beyond the outer end of every long pad that points out of the part. pads: the boxes of its SMD pads. A part with fewer than five pads, or lane 0, keeps its box.
- class `Placer`: Places the footprints of a Board that has its outline.
  - `.__init__(board, nets=None, planes=('GND',), gap=0.2, edge=0.5, back_cost=2.5, lane=0.0)`: 
  - `.shape(ref, rot, back)`: (courtyard (x0, y0, x1, y1), {pad: [(dx, dy)]}, through) relative to the part's origin.
  - `.area(ref)`: 
  - `.free(ref, x, y, rot=0, back=False)`: True if ref fits at (x, y): on the board, allowed by the rules, clear of placed parts.
  - `.keep(x0, y0, x1, y1, sides='F')`: Reserve a rectangle on the front ("F"), the back ("B") or both ("FB"), e.g. for a hand route.
  - `.inside(refs, *rects)`: Each of refs must lie with its whole courtyard inside one of the rectangles (x0, y0, x1, y1); None for a side that is open. Called again for a part, both hold.
  - `.only(refs, side)`: refs go on the front ("F") or on the back ("B") only.
  - `.fix(ref, x, y, rot=0, back=False)`: Put ref's footprint origin at (x, y). Rules, gap and edge do not apply: you decided.
  - `.centre(ref, cx, cy, rot=0, back=False)`: Put the middle of ref's courtyard at (cx, cy): handy for connectors, whose origin is pin 1.
  - `.pad(ref, pad)`: Position of a placed part's pad.
  - `.find(net, other=None, block=None)`: The one two-pad part between `net` and `other`, or the one on `net` whose group name (a string, or a tuple ending in one) starts with `block`.
  - `.face(ref, net, side, back=False)`: The rotation that puts ref's pad on `net` towards "left", "right", "up" or "down".
  - `.cost(ref, x, y, rot, back, anchor, pull)`: (cost in mm of wire, supply pins this spot would decouple). Lower is better.
  - `.near(ref, anchor, start=None, sides=(False, True), rots=(0, 90, 180, 270), pull=0.15, step=0.5, reach=3.0, max_r=45.0)`: Place ref at the cheapest free spot around `start` (default: where its nets pull it).
  - `.big(ref)`: Placed in the first pass: everything but small resistors, capacitors and test points.
  - `.linked(ref)`: How many of ref's signal pads have a placed counterpart.
  - `.large(refs, anchor, sides=(False,), pull=0.6)`: First pass: the unplaced large parts of refs around anchor, largest first.
  - `.small(refs, anchor, sides=(False, True), pull=0.15)`: Second pass: the rest of refs, always the part with the most connections to placed parts.
  - `.length()`: Half-perimeter wire length of all nets off the planes, mm: a figure to compare placements by.
  - `.problem(moves=20000, seed=1, weights=None)`: The placement as a problem for `leiterbahn-route place` (router/src/place.rs).
  - `.improve(tmp_dir, moves=20000, seed=1, weights=None, say=None)`: Move, turn and exchange the parts near() placed where that shortens the connections.

## `leiterbahn.kicad.planar`

Plane geometry without pcbnew: the smallest gap between two groups of copper shapes, and the contours and rectangle tests behind board cut-outs.

- `seg_dist(a, b)`: Distance between two segments (x1, y1, x2, y2); a segment with equal ends is a point.
- class `Shape`: A piece of copper: edges (x1, y1, x2, y2) grown by radius r, or a filled polygon (r = 0).
  - `.__init__(edges, r, rings, tag)`: 
  - `.contains(x, y)`: Point inside a polygon shape (even-odd over all rings, so holes count as outside).
- `capsule(ax, ay, bx, by, r, tag=None)`: A track: the segment a-b with round ends, r = half its width.
- `circle(x, y, r, tag=None)`: A via or round pad.
- `polygon(rings, tag=None)`: A filled polygon; rings are lists of (x, y), the outline first, then any holes.
- `closest(shapes_a, shapes_b)`: Smallest gap between any shape of group a and any shape of group b.
- `rects_overlap(a, b, margin=0.0)`: True if rectangles (x0, y0, x1, y1) come closer than margin (0: they overlap).
- `rounded_rect(x, y, w, h, r=0.0)`: Closed contour of a rectangle with corner radius r, as a list of ("seg", start, end) and ("arc", start, mid, end); zero-length sides are left out.
- `cutout_side(box, rect, corner=0.0, tol=1e-06)`: Where a rectangular cut-out sits on a board: "slot" (wholly inside) or the side it opens onto ("left", "right", "top", "bottom": a notch).
- `notch(contour, box, rect, r=0.0)`: Cut a notch into a board contour (as made by rounded_rect).
